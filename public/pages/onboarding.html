How Splitr works
1
Set your monthly income
Tell Splitr how much you earn each month. This becomes the pool your sections are funded from.
2
Create sections
Name your spending categories — Food, Transport, Entertainment, Bills, or anything else that fits your life.
3
Allocate budgets
Give each section a monthly budget. Splitr tracks how much you have left in each one.
4
Track spending
Log transactions and see them instantly deducted from the right section.
5
Know what's left
At any moment, see exactly how much you can still spend in each section — before you spend it.
